# Comparative Matrix

| Feature/Aspect         | Indrive                                  | Amana (Poste Maroc)                          | Glovo                                     |
| :--------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| **Main Service**       | Ride-hailing, item delivery              | National & International Parcel/Document Delivery | Food delivery, multi-category delivery    |
| **UX/UI Structure**    | Focus on negotiation, simple interface   | Functional, but user reviews suggest responsiveness issues | Intuitive, visually driven, clear order flow |
| **Pricing/Monetization** | User-driven pricing (negotiation), low commission for drivers | Standard postal rates, government-backed     | Commission on orders, delivery fees, subscription (Glovo Prime) |
| **Services Offered**   | Rides, courier services                  | Parcel, document delivery                    | Food, groceries, pharmacy, anything delivery |
| **Strengths**          | Price flexibility, driver empowerment, competitive pricing | Established national infrastructure, trust as postal service | Wide range of offerings, strong brand in food delivery, real-time tracking, loyalty programs |
| **Weaknesses**         | Inconsistent pricing, potential for longer wait times | Digital platform reliability issues, slower compared to private couriers | High commission for restaurants, potential for high delivery fees for users |
| **Installs/Ratings**   | High installs (millions), mixed reviews (due to notifications/fare changes) | 100K+ installs (My Amana app), moderate reviews (2.5-2.6 stars) | High installs (millions), generally positive reviews |
